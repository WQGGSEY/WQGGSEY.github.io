About

I am an undergraduate at KAIST, majoring in the School of Computing with a double major in the Department of Mathematical Sciences. I am broadly interested in the theoretical foundations of machine learning, in particular representation learning and the dynamics of how it is learned. I am currently doing undergraduate research on the theory of contrastive learning in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at KAIST.

On the industry side, I have worked on signal research and execution systems in quantitative finance at Timefolio Asset Management.
